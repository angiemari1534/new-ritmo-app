========================================
  RITMO — Spanish Through Songs (mobile app)
========================================

Everything for the new phone app lives in this folder:
  C:\Users\luism\Ritmo\

  • RitmoApp\        <- the phone app (iPhone + Android)
  • ritmo-backend\   <- the little server that holds your secret API keys

Your old web player is untouched on your Desktop; this is the new, real app.


────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT WORKS RIGHT NOW
────────────────────────────────────────
• The full app is built: Library, Create, Generating, and Player screens.
• The backend is built and tested (it runs and answers correctly).
• To make it generate REAL songs, we just need to (1) add your two keys and
  (2) run it on your phone. Steps below.


────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Add your secret keys (one time)
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Open the folder:  C:\Users\luism\Ritmo\ritmo-backend\
2. Find the file  ".env.example"  and make a COPY of it named exactly  ".env"
   (just ".env", no other name).
3. Open ".env" in Notepad and paste your keys after the = signs:
       MINIMAX_API_KEY=your_minimax_key
       ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=your_elevenlabs_key
4. Save and close. This file stays on your computer and is never shared.


────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 — Start the server
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Open the app "Terminal" or "PowerShell".
2. Copy-paste this and press Enter:
       cd C:\Users\luism\Ritmo\ritmo-backend
       npm start
3. You should see:  "Ritmo backend listening..."  and both keys "set".
   Leave this window open while you use the app.


────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 — Point the app at your computer
────────────────────────────────────────
1. In PowerShell, run:  ipconfig
   Find the line "IPv4 Address" — it looks like 192.168.1.20
2. Open this file in Notepad:
       C:\Users\luism\Ritmo\RitmoApp\src\config.ts
3. Change the address to YOUR number, keeping :3000 at the end, e.g.:
       export const BACKEND_URL = "http://192.168.1.20:3000";
4. Save.

   (Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi for this to work.)


────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 4 — Run the app on your phone
────────────────────────────────────────
1. On your phone, install "Expo Go" from the App Store / Google Play (free).
2. On your computer, open a SECOND PowerShell window and run:
       cd C:\Users\luism\Ritmo\RitmoApp
       npx expo start
3. A QR code appears. On iPhone use the Camera app to scan it; on Android
   scan it from inside the Expo Go app.
4. Ritmo opens on your phone! Tap a starter song or "Create a song."
